What Classica does

Grammar Engine

Generates and parses declensions, conjugations, participles, infinitives, pronouns, and irregular forms.

Practice & Drills

Fill-in-the-blank, table completion, multiple choice, timed drills, and free response with adaptive difficulty.

Translation

Latin ↔ English with syntax highlighting and grammar feedback on every sentence.

Vocabulary

SM-2 spaced repetition, mastery tracking, and frequency-ranked, categorized word lists.

Reading

Annotated passages with tap-to-parse, contextual notes, and comprehension questions.

Scansion

Syllabification, vowel-quantity marking, elision detection, and dactylic-hexameter analysis.

Our Mission

At Classica, we believe that learning Latin should be accessible to everyone.

Too often, students are faced with expensive textbooks, scattered online resources, and learning tools filled with advertisements or locked behind subscriptions. We created Classica to offer a different experience—a free, ad-free platform designed to help anyone learn Latin with confidence.

Whether you're beginning your first Latin course, preparing for exams, teaching in the classroom, or exploring the language out of personal interest, Classica provides clear explanations, interactive practice, and thoughtfully designed learning tools to support your journey.

Our mission is simple: to make high-quality Latin education available to everyone, regardless of experience or financial barriers. We are committed to continually improving Classica by listening to our community, expanding our content, and building the tools that learners truly need.

Latin has shaped languages, literature, law, science, and history for thousands of years. We hope Classica helps make that rich tradition more approachable, more engaging, and more accessible for learners around the world.

Thank you for being part of our journey.
