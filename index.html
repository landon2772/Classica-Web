What Classica does

Grammar Engine

Generates and parses declensions, conjugations, participles, infinitives, pronouns, and irregular forms.

Practice & Drills

Fill-in-the-blank, table completion, multiple choice, timed drills, and free response with adaptive difficulty.

Translation

Latin ↔ English with syntax highlighting and grammar feedback on every sentence.

Vocabulary

SM-2 spaced repetition, mastery tracking, and frequency-ranked, categorized word lists.

Reading

Annotated passages with tap-to-parse, contextual notes, and comprehension questions.

Scansion

Syllabification, vowel-quantity marking, elision detection, and dactylic-hexameter analysis.
